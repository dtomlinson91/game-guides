# Welt builds and teams

Welt is a 5★ Imaginary character on the Path of Nihility. He is a standard
banner character, and a Novaflare rework in patch 4.2 changed his kit
completely. This guide covers two uses: Welt as the sustain in a sustainless
Aventurine • Waveflair and Ashveil team, and Welt as the second Nihility unit in
an Acheron team. It also compares him against Robin • Summeretto for the Acheron
slot.

Written for version 4.5, on 2026-09-20.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Welt has two separate builds, and they share almost no main stats. A support
> Welt runs Effect Hit Rate, SPD, HP% and Energy Regen Rate. A damage Welt runs
> CRIT Rate, ATK%, Imaginary DMG and ATK%. Prydwen states that you "will not get
> by in those roles running your main DPS setup, regardless of your Eidolon
> level." Use a relic loadout for each. See [Welt's two builds](#welts-two-builds).

> [!CAUTION]
> Welt is the hardest character in this repository to pilot. Prydwen lists
> "exceedingly difficult character to play properly" as his first weakness, and
> the sustain role as the hardest of his roles. The whole kit turns on **when**
> you press his Ultimate. Read [Core loop](#core-loop) before either team sheet.

## Pick a role

Use this table to choose the build and the team. The two teams want different
things from Welt, and one of his traces is worth nothing in one of them.

| Question | Waveflair team | Acheron team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welt's job | Replace the healer with delays | Second Nihility unit, plus DEF shred |
| Build | Support | Support |
| A2 damage buff useful | **No**, Waveflair deals Elation DMG | Yes, Acheron deals Lightning DMG |
| Needs Effect Hit Rate | For the Slow, and for his own ATK | Yes, his debuffs feed Slashed Dream |
| Sustainless | Yes, that is the point | Optional |
| Prydwen top MoC rank with this DPS | Not ranked | Rank 180 (0.15% appearance) |

> [!WARNING]
> Neither team appears near the top of Welt's field data. Nine of Welt's ten
> ranked Memory of Chaos teams pair him with **Himeko Nova**, and his best team
> sits at rank 21 with a 2.13% appearance rate. No Waveflair team appears at all.
> One Acheron team appears, at rank 180. The r/WeltMains subreddit shows the
> same: across the 50 most recent threads, Himeko Nova appears at least seven
> times, and Acheron and Waveflair appear zero times. Both teams below are built
> from kit text and reviewer advice, not from field results.

## Core loop

Welt's kit turns on one debuff, applied by one ability. Read this before either
team sheet, because every later section uses these terms.

- **Weightless** — applied to all enemies by his Ultimate. It is **guaranteed**,
  with no base chance to resist. It lasts 2 turns.
- **Imprison** — also applied by his Ultimate, at 100% base chance, for 1 turn.
  It delays the target's action by 12% and cuts SPD by 10%.
- **Slow** — applied by his Skill, at 75% base chance per hit, for 2 turns. It
  cuts SPD by 10%.

Weightless does four things at once.

1. Enemies under it take **40% reduced DEF** and 5% reduced SPD.
2. Every hit on a Weightless enemy **delays its action by 4%**. This triggers up
   to 8 times per target per enemy turn.
3. Every ally attack on a Weightless enemy grants that ally **+10% DMG dealt**,
   stacking to 10 times for **+100%**, lasting 2 turns and refreshing on each
   attack. This is his A2 trace, Retribution.
4. At Eidolon 4, enemies under it lose **30% All-Type RES**.

His other numbers:

| Ability | Output |
| --- | --- |
| Skill | 5 bounces of 72% ATK, and his Talent fires per hit for 100% ATK each. About 860% ATK total, or about 946% with the A4 trace. |
| Ultimate | 150% ATK to all enemies, at a cost of 120 Energy. He starts each battle with 30 Energy from his A2. |
| Talent | Attacking an already-Slowed enemy adds 100% ATK of Additional DMG. |
| A6 | Effect Hit Rate above 40% converts to ATK, at +20% ATK per 10% over, capped at +80% ATK at 80% Effect Hit Rate. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Use the Ultimate immediately after an enemy's turn ends.** That is the point
> in the turn order where the delay pushes them back furthest, and each later
> delay compounds from there. Prydwen calls this "the single most important thing
> about playing Welt properly". Pressing it at any other time wastes most of the
> effect.

> [!TIP]
> You get 2 turns of Weightless. You can let an enemy act once or twice and
> reapply Weightless before your team attacks again. Holding the Ultimate for the
> right moment beats pressing it on cooldown.

> [!CAUTION]
> Three fights punish the delay rather than reward it. First, bosses whose
> mechanics need them to act, such as ones that spawn adds you ramp off. Second,
> bosses with untargetable parts, which Weightless cannot reach. Third, bosses
> that act immediately under their own conditions. Check whether you **want** to
> delay the enemy before you build a team around delaying it.

## Waveflair team

This team drops the healer. Welt replaces it by delaying enemies so hard that
they rarely act. Prydwen's Aventurine • Waveflair review names it directly:
"playing sustainless and keeping Welt to construct Team Hubby is obviously very
good for very obvious Welt-shaped reasons."

**Roster:** Aventurine • Waveflair, Ashveil, Mortenax Blade, Welt.

| Slot | Character | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main DPS | Aventurine • Waveflair | Quantum Elation damage |
| Specialist | Ashveil | 40% DEF reduction, 120% CRIT DMG on ally Follow-up ATK |
| Amplifier | Mortenax Blade | DEF reduction, Vulnerability, taunt and self-healing |
| Sustain replacement | Welt | 40% DEF reduction, Imprison, stacked delays |

> [!CAUTION]
> **Welt's A2 damage buff is worth nothing to Waveflair.** Elation DMG does not
> take generic DMG% boosts. Prydwen states this twice, once in the Welt review
> ("For Elation teammates, it's a completely dead Trace") and once in the
> Mortenax Blade review ("As ever, Elation DMG doesn't care about generic DMG%
> boosts"). The buff still applies to Ashveil, who deals Lightning DMG, and to
> Mortenax Blade, who deals Fire DMG. Do not count it for your main damage
> dealer.

What Welt still supplies to this team:

1. **40% DEF reduction on all enemies.** This stacks with Ashveil's 40% and with
   Mortenax Blade's, and DEF reduction is one of the few amplifiers that gets
   better the more you have.
2. **The delays.** This is the sustain. Imprison for 1 turn, plus 4% per hit up
   to 8 times per enemy turn. A team of frequent attackers pushes enemies down
   the turn order repeatedly.
3. **30% All-Type RES reduction at Eidolon 4.** This one does help Elation
   damage, unlike the A2.

> [!WARNING]
> Adding Welt **downgrades Mortenax Blade's A6 trace**. That trace reads: while
> his Zone is active, all allies deal 50% more DMG, and "if there are other
> Nihility characters aside from Mortenax Blade in the ally team, Ultimate DMG
> dealt by ally targets increases by 75%. Otherwise, DMG dealt by Mortenax Blade
> additionally increases by 75%." Welt is Nihility, so his presence converts
> Blade's personal +75% into a team-wide Ultimate DMG buff. Waveflair's Ultimate
> is a small part of his output, so the team gains little and Blade loses a lot.
> Prydwen's Blade review calls the Welt pairing "definitely a worse option but
> it's two of the best generalist units on one team, so it's still going to be
> pretty good."

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Prydwen names Welt **or** Mortenax Blade for the sustainless slot, not both as
> a requirement. The Blade review states: "if you're really feeling like going
> without a sustain for Elation and your Welt is occupied elsewhere, Blade is
> going to be a surprisingly excellent option for the 4th slot, especially since
> he's very tanky and draws a lot of the enemies' attention to himself." If you
> run both, accept the A6 downgrade as the price.

> [!NOTE]
> This trade-off is derived from the trace text and from the two reviews. No
> source measures a sustainless Waveflair team with and without Welt. Treat the
> ranking of Welt against Blade for that slot as untested.

If you want the healer back instead, use Hyacine. She is fast, her memosprite
attacks whenever she acts, and both of those feed Ashveil's Charge and
Waveflair's Fervor. That team is covered in
[`ashveil-builds-and-teams.md`](ashveil-builds-and-teams.md).

## Acheron team

Here Welt's Path matters more than his kit. Acheron's A4 trace reads: "When there
are 1 or 2 Nihility characters other than Acheron in the team, the DMG dealt by
Acheron's Basic ATK, Skill, and Ultimate increases to 115% or 160% of the
original DMG respectively."

**Roster:** Acheron, Mortenax Blade, Welt, sustain or a fourth amplifier.

| Slot | Character | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main DPS | Acheron | Lightning Nihility, Ultimate-focused |
| Nihility 1 | Mortenax Blade | Prydwen calls him "the absolute best-in-slot support for Acheron without question" |
| Nihility 2 | Welt | 40% DEF reduction, up to 100% DMG buff, delays |
| Flex | Hyacine, Dan Heng • Permansor Terrae, Sparkle | Sustain, or a fourth amplifier for a sustainless run |

Welt does four things for Acheron.

1. **He completes the second Nihility slot.** Going from one Nihility ally to two
   raises her multiplier from 115% to 160%. That is a **1.39× increase** on her
   Basic ATK, Skill and Ultimate damage.
2. **He flips Mortenax Blade's A6 the useful way.** With Welt present, Blade's
   trace grants **all allies +75% Ultimate DMG** instead of granting Blade
   himself +75%. Acheron's damage is almost entirely her Ultimate, so this is the
   opposite of the Waveflair case. Prydwen's Blade review calls this "the final
   part of the Acheron-specific turbo synergy."
3. **He supplies 40% DEF reduction**, plus 30% All-Type RES reduction at
   Eidolon 4.
4. **His A2 works here.** Acheron deals Lightning DMG, so the stacking buff up to
   +100% applies in full.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Welt is **not** a good Slashed Dream generator. Prydwen states plainly that
> "he is not best at generating stacks for Acheron." His debuffs come mostly from
> one Ultimate every few turns, and Acheron gains only 1 point per ability usage.
> Mortenax Blade is the stack engine in this team, because his taunt applies a
> debuff whenever an enemy attacks him.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Do not run Welt with Jiaoqiu.** Prydwen names the conflict directly: Welt's
> delay "can cause some anti-synergy with Jiaoqiu, who is still Acheron's best
> Slashed Dream generator at E0, owing to the fact that he requires enemies
> taking action while Welt is actively preventing it." The same logic applies to
> any stack source that needs enemy turns, including Mortenax Blade's taunt. Welt
> reduces the number of enemy turns that generate your stacks.

> [!WARNING]
> Welt appears in exactly **one** of Acheron's ten ranked Memory of Chaos teams,
> at rank 180 with a 0.15% appearance rate: Acheron, Mortenax Blade, Tribbie,
> Welt. Mortenax Blade appears in all ten. The field plays Acheron with Blade
> plus Tribbie, Ashveil or Cipher. Welt is a valid choice on paper, not a
> popular one.

## Welt or Robin

This section answers the direct comparison. The two characters are not
alternatives in the same sense, because one changes a multiplier and the other
adds buffs.

| | Welt | Robin • Summeretto |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Element and Path | Imaginary, **Nihility** | Wind, **Remembrance** |
| Counts for Acheron's A4 | **Yes** | **No** |
| Flips Mortenax Blade's A6 | **Yes**, to team Ultimate DMG +75% | No |
| DEF effect | DEF **reduction** 40% | DEF **ignore**, 40% plus Vibes × 0.5% |
| Damage buff | Up to +100% DMG dealt, ramped | ATK from her Max HP, or CRIT DMG +40% plus Vibes × 1.5% |
| Other | Imprison, Slow, action delay, 30% RES reduction at E4 | Action advance 100%, fixed Energy, enemies take more DMG, +20% All-Type RES PEN at E2 |
| Scaling stat | ATK, with Effect Hit Rate converted to ATK | Max HP |

**The structural answer.** If Robin • Summeretto occupies a slot that would
otherwise hold a Nihility unit, Acheron drops from two Nihility allies to one.
Her A4 multiplier falls from 160% to 115%, and she also loses the +75% team
Ultimate DMG from Mortenax Blade's A6. Those two losses compound on the ability
that does nearly all her damage.

**The case for Robin • Summeretto anyway.** She is the stronger raw buffer. Her
Ultimate advances an ally's action by 100% and refunds a percentage of their max
Energy, which matters for a 9-point Slashed Dream gate. Her Zone applies DEF
ignore that stacks with Welt's DEF reduction rather than competing with it. Her
A2 gives Acheron CRIT DMG, because Acheron's ATK is higher than Robin's.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Robin • Summeretto may generate no Slashed Dream at all.** Acheron gains a
> point when "any unit inflicts debuffs on an enemy target while using their
> ability". Robin's Zone grants allies **DEF ignore**, which is a buff on the
> ally, not a debuff on the enemy. Her memosprite passively raises enemy DMG
> taken while the Songbirds are on the field, which is an aura rather than an
> ability usage. This guide could not confirm whether either registers. Check it
> in game by watching her Slashed Dream counter, because if she generates
> nothing, the Ultimate gate becomes the limiting factor.

> [!NOTE]
> Neither guide site compares these two. Prydwen's Acheron page last updated its
> profile on 31 May 2026, months before Robin • Summeretto released, and does not
> mention her. Robin • Summeretto's own page, updated 12 September 2026, does not
> mention Acheron in any synergy entry. Across the 50 most recent threads on
> r/AcheronMainsHSR, neither Welt nor Robin • Summeretto appears in a title. The
> comparison above is derived from trace text.

**What this guide recommends.** Run Welt in the Acheron team when your other
Nihility slot is filled by Mortenax Blade and you want the 160% multiplier. Keep
Robin • Summeretto for a team where the Path does not gate a multiplier, such as
March 7th • Evernight, Castorice, or the Ashveil and Mortenax Blade team. If you
want both, the Nihility count is what decides, not the buff quality.

## Common mistakes

Check this list when a Welt team underperforms and the build looks correct.

1. **Pressing the Ultimate on cooldown.** Press it right after an enemy's turn
   ends. See [Core loop](#core-loop).
2. **Counting the A2 buff for an Elation damage dealer.** It does nothing for
   Elation DMG. See [Waveflair team](#waveflair-team).
3. **Running a damage build in a support slot.** The main stats barely overlap.
   See [Welt's two builds](#welts-two-builds).
4. **Pairing Welt with Jiaoqiu in an Acheron team.** Welt removes the enemy turns
   that Jiaoqiu needs.
5. **Delaying a boss whose mechanic you want.** Some fights need the enemy to
   act.
6. **Forgetting the Energy you no longer take.** Welt's delays stop enemies
   hitting you, and being hit is a real Energy source. Prydwen lists this as the
   one problem that skill alone does not fix. Build rotations that fund their own
   Energy.
7. **Skipping Effect Hit Rate.** Below 40% Effect Hit Rate his A6 gives no ATK at
   all, and his Skill Slow lands at only 75% base chance.

## Stat targets

Use these while gearing. Effect Hit Rate is the stat people skip and should not.

| Stat | Target | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Effect Hit Rate | 70% to 80% | 67% lands a 100% base chance debuff. Above 40% it converts to ATK, capping at +80% ATK at 80% |
| SPD | 124 minimum, 160 preferred for support | One r/WeltMains player gives these numbers. Prydwen recommends SPD boots for support Welt |
| ATK | 2200 to 2600+ | Prydwen's figure |
| CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG | Damage build only | Prydwen: "Only invest in them if you're playing Welt as DPS" |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> SPD matters more for Welt than for most supports, and it works in both
> directions. A faster Welt reaches his next Ultimate sooner, which keeps
> Weightless up. Faster allies attack more, which both stacks his A2 faster and
> delays enemies more. Prydwen calls SPD "disproportionately important for Welt,
> specifically."

> [!WARNING]
> If Weightless drops, you lose the 40% DEF reduction, the delays, and the
> stacked A2 buff at the same time. The buff has a 2-turn grace period after the
> last attack, but a Welt who cannot reapply Weightless in time loses three
> effects at once.

## Team rebuilds

This section answers whether adding Welt changes how you build the rest of the
team. The short answer is that Acheron does not change and two other things do.

| Character | Change when Welt joins |
| --- | --- |
| Acheron | **No relic or stat change.** Keep Pioneer Diver of Dead Waters 4-piece with Izumo Gensei and Takama Divine Realm 2-piece, ATK 2900 to 3200+, CRIT Rate 80% to 90%+, CRIT DMG 160% to 180%+ |
| Acheron SPD | Prefer **134+** over 101. Derived, not measured — see the note below |
| Mortenax Blade | No relic change, but do not build him as a hypercarry. His A6 now buffs the team's Ultimate DMG instead of his own damage |
| Sustain | If you keep one, equip "Trend of the Universal Market" for a chance to apply debuffs during enemy actions, which generates Slashed Dream |
| Jiaoqiu | Remove him. See [Common mistakes](#common-mistakes) |

> [!NOTE]
> The Acheron SPD recommendation is reasoning, not a measured result. Welt's
> delays cut the number of enemy turns. Mortenax Blade's taunt generates Slashed
> Dream when enemies attack him, so fewer enemy turns means fewer stacks from
> that source. More Acheron turns partly replace them. Prydwen gives her SPD as
> "101 or 134+" without reference to Welt. Test it on your own account before
> rerolling relics for it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Robin • Summeretto needs no rebuild for an Acheron team either. Her stats are
> HP 6000 to 8000, SPD 160+, Energy Regen Rate 119.44%, with
> World-Remaking Deliverer 4-piece and Amphoreus, The Eternal Land 2-piece. Her
> problem in an Acheron team is her Path, not her build.

## Eidolon value

Read this once when planning pulls. Welt is a standard banner character, so
Eidolons accumulate over time without targeted spending.

| Eidolon | Effect | Worth it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Extra 60% ATK hit on Skill and Ultimate against Weightless targets | Minor |
| E2 | 3 Energy per Talent trigger. Affected by Energy Regen Rate | Large for uptime. His Skill triggers it up to 5 times, so about 15 Energy per Skill |
| E4 | Weightless enemies lose 30% All-Type RES | **His strongest Eidolon by far.** Prydwen puts the gain at 30% to 33% damage, and it is the only Eidolon that helps an Elation team |
| E6 | CRIT Rate +30% and CRIT DMG +60% against Slowed enemies | Does not affect his Talent, his highest multiplier. Prydwen flags this as possibly a bug |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> E4 is the breakpoint that changes his rating. Prydwen's review says Welt is not
> best-in-slot for any team "not until E4 anyway", and calls E4 Welt for an
> Elation team "a no-contest choice". If you are deciding whether to spend
> standard selectors or Golden Companion Spirits, E4 is the target.
