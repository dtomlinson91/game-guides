# Frost Death Knight — Mythic+ rotation

A combat reference for Frost Death Knight in Mythic+, for Midnight, patch 12.1,
Season 2. It assumes the **Deathbringer** hero talent and the dual-wield
Breath of Sindragosa build, which all 16 sampled logs played. Every rate and
timing comes from 16 Warcraft Logs keys at key level 16 to 18, not from a
written guide [[1]](#ref-1). Where the logs and the guide sites disagree, this
file follows the logs and says so.

> [!NOTE]
> All 16 sampled players wore the 4-piece tier set. The set changes only
> Remorseless Winter, which Pillar of Frost casts automatically, so the buttons
> and their order are the same without it. See
> [Without the tier set](#without-the-tier-set).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Two terms appear in every list below. **Killing Machine** is a proc that
> makes the next Obliterate or Frostscythe a guaranteed critical strike, and it
> stacks to 2 [[9]](#ref-9). **Rime** is a proc that makes the next Howling
> Blast free and much stronger [[11]](#ref-11).
> [How Frost works](#how-frost-works) explains both.

## How Frost works

Frost runs on two resources and two cooldown clocks. The procs decide each
press, and the clocks decide when the burst happens.

| System | What it is | How it refills or resets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Runes** | Spent by Obliterate and Frostscythe (2 each), Howling Blast and Reaper's Mark [[17]](#ref-17)[[7]](#ref-7) | Recharge over time. Horn of Winter is now passive and returns 2 Runes when all are spent, once every 45 seconds [[14]](#ref-14) |
| **Runic Power** | Spent by Frost Strike and Glacial Advance (35 each) and Breath of Sindragosa (60) [[17]](#ref-17)[[4]](#ref-4) | Generated by Rune spenders. Empower Rune Weapon gives 40 at once [[3]](#ref-3) |
| **Killing Machine** | Next Obliterate or Frostscythe always crits. Up to 2 stacks [[9]](#ref-9) | Granted by Empower Rune Weapon, Reaper's Mark and other sources [[3]](#ref-3)[[8]](#ref-8). Obliterate consumes **all** stacks at once, through Killing Streak [[10]](#ref-10) |
| **Rime** | Next Howling Blast costs no Rune and deals 275% more damage [[11]](#ref-11) | 45% chance from each Frost Strike or Glacial Advance [[11]](#ref-11) |
| **45-second clock** | Pillar of Frost (+20% Strength, 12 seconds) and Reaper's Mark [[2]](#ref-2)[[7]](#ref-7) | Both have a 45-second cooldown |
| **90-second clock** | Breath of Sindragosa, Frostwyrm's Fury and Raise Dead [[4]](#ref-4)[[5]](#ref-5) | Every second Pillar. Breath lasts 8 seconds, plus 0.8 seconds for each Killing Machine or Rime consumed [[4]](#ref-4) |

Everything in this table is temporary. Buffs and procs expire, and nothing
carries from one pull to the next except cooldowns. The two clocks are the
only thing a player plans ahead. Everything else is a reaction to a proc.

> [!TIP]
> Breath of Sindragosa is no longer a channel that drains Runic Power. In patch
> 12.1 it costs 60 Runic Power once and then extends itself on procs
> [[4]](#ref-4). Frost Strike stays in the rotation while it is up.

## Single target

Press the first line that is ready. This covers a boss or any pull with up to
three enemies.

1. **Pillar of Frost + Reaper's Mark** — one press, every 45 seconds. Every
   second press also casts Breath of Sindragosa, Raise Dead and the on-use
   trinket. See [The burst window](#the-burst-window)
2. **Frostwyrm's Fury** — about 2 seconds after a Breath press, then recall it
   about 3.5 seconds later. See [Frostwyrm's Fury](#frostwyrms-fury)
3. **Empower Rune Weapon** — whenever a charge is ready. It costs no global
   cooldown
4. **Obliterate** — at 2 Killing Machine stacks
5. **Howling Blast** — with Rime, and only with Rime
6. **Obliterate** — at 1 Killing Machine stack, if Runes allow
7. **Frost Strike** — everything else

The proc state at each press shows the order directly. The table covers 2,267
presses on boss pulls with one to three enemies [[1]](#ref-1).

| Killing Machine | Rime | What the field pressed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | no | Obliterate 79%, Frost Strike 20% |
| 2 | yes | Obliterate 59%, Howling Blast 36% |
| 1 | yes | Howling Blast 74%, Obliterate 22% |
| 0 | yes | Howling Blast 93% |
| 1 | no | Obliterate 53%, Frost Strike 44% |
| 0 | no | Frost Strike 85%, Obliterate 12% |

> [!NOTE]
> Line 6 is a judgement call, not a rule. At one Killing Machine stack the
> field splits almost evenly between Obliterate and Frost Strike. Icy Veins
> gives the condition: spend a single stack only with 3 or more Runes
> [[19]](#ref-19). The logs carry no Rune count, so this file cannot check it.

## AoE

The skeleton is the same. Two buttons swap, and the cooldowns do not change.

1. **Pillar of Frost + Reaper's Mark** — same press, same timing
2. **Frostwyrm's Fury** — same timing
3. **Empower Rune Weapon** — whenever a charge is ready
4. **Frostscythe** — replaces Obliterate, at 2 Killing Machine stacks first
5. **Howling Blast** — with Rime
6. **Glacial Advance** — replaces Frost Strike

On trash pulls with 4 or more enemies, the field casts Frostscythe 12.3 times
a minute and Glacial Advance 9.9 times, against 3.8 Obliterates and 3.9 Frost
Strikes [[1]](#ref-1).

> [!TIP]
> Learn one list. The AoE rotation is the single-target rotation with
> Frostscythe for Obliterate and Glacial Advance for Frost Strike.

## When to swap

The swap is gradual. The share of each AoE button rises with the size of the
pull [[1]](#ref-1).

| Enemies in the pull | Pulls | Frostscythe share of Rune spenders | Glacial Advance share of Runic Power spenders |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 | 0% | 5% |
| 2 to 3 | 19 | 3% | 7% |
| 4 to 6 | 17 | 25% | 26% |
| 7 to 9 | 27 | 28% | 32% |
| 10 to 24 | 61 | 48% | 47% |
| 25 or more | 42 | 70% | 69% |

At two or three enemies the field stays on Obliterate and Frost Strike. The
two AoE buttons take over only in large packs.

> [!WARNING]
> These counts are distinct enemies seen across a whole pull, not enemies
> alive at the same moment. The data therefore **cannot** give an exact "swap
> at N targets" rule. Read it as a direction: single-target buttons on small
> groups, AoE buttons on real packs, and a mix in between.

## The burst window

The rotation runs on a 45-second clock. The field presses Pillar of Frost a
median 47.4 seconds apart, against a hard floor of 45.0 seconds, for 26 to 35
Pillars per key [[1]](#ref-1). Every second Pillar is a larger press that adds
the 90-second cooldowns.

| Press | How often | Windows in sample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Breath press** — Pillar, Reaper's Mark, Breath, Raise Dead, trinket | Every 90 seconds | 222 |
| **Pillar-only press** — Pillar, Reaper's Mark | Between Breath presses | 276 |

Across the sample, Breath is up 25% of combat time, Pillar alone another 13%,
and neither for the other 62% [[1]](#ref-1).

### The Breath press

Five casts land within a fraction of a second. Times are seconds after the
Pillar of Frost press, as medians across 222 windows [[1]](#ref-1).

| Time | Press | In how many windows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| −0.1 s | Empower Rune Weapon | 98% |
| 0.0 s | Pillar of Frost, Reaper's Mark, Raise Dead | 100%, 100%, 88% |
| 0.0 s | Breath of Sindragosa | 100% |
| +0.9 s | First Obliterate or Frostscythe | — |
| +2.0 s | Frostwyrm's Fury | 100% |
| +2.1 s | First Howling Blast | 98% |
| +5.8 s | Frostwyrm's Fury recall | 91% |

Empower Rune Weapon comes just before the press for two reasons. It gives 40
Runic Power toward the 60 that Breath costs [[3]](#ref-3)[[4]](#ref-4). Breath
also returns one Empower Rune Weapon charge when it starts, so a charge spent
first is not wasted [[4]](#ref-4).

The field puts 63 of 68 potions and 151 of 166 Voracious Heart of Ula'tek
presses into a Breath window [[1]](#ref-1).

### The Pillar-only press

Pillar of Frost and Reaper's Mark go out together, and nothing else is held
for them. Times are medians across 276 windows [[1]](#ref-1).

| Time | Press | In how many windows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0 s | Pillar of Frost, Reaper's Mark | 100%, 99% |
| +0.8 s to +1.1 s | First Frostscythe or Obliterate | — |
| +2.0 s | Empower Rune Weapon | 84% |

Breath and Frostwyrm's Fury appear in only 7% of these windows. The field does
not hold Pillar of Frost to wait for Breath.

> [!NOTE]
> One player reports a strict rule of "send 45 with 1.5": the 45-second
> cooldowns always go with the 90-second ones when both are ready. The same
> player sends Pillar alone when Breath is 30 seconds or more away
> [[33]](#ref-33). The logs agree with both halves.

## Frostwyrm's Fury

Frostwyrm's Fury is a 90-second cooldown. The Apex talent, Chosen of
Frostbrood, changes it in three ways [[6]](#ref-6):

- Each cast extends an active Pillar of Frost by 2 seconds per point
- It grants 15% Haste for 12 seconds
- The fourth point lets the player cast it a second time within 45 seconds,
  to **recall** the wyrm at 50% effectiveness

The field casts it only inside a Breath window: 241 of 242 casts near a Pillar
come after it, at a median of +2.0 seconds [[1]](#ref-1). The recall follows a
median 3.5 seconds after the first cast, and 231 of 244 recalls land inside the
same Pillar of Frost [[1]](#ref-1).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Recall the wyrm as soon as it is available.** The buff "Chosen of
> Frostbrood" (1265639) reads "You may recall your Frostwyrm" [[6]](#ref-6). In
> the logs it lasts a median 1.9 seconds before the recall consumes it
> [[1]](#ref-1). The written guides disagree with this timing. See
> [Where logs beat guides](#where-logs-beat-guides).

The first cast costs a 1.5-second global cooldown [[5]](#ref-5). The recall
does not. The next global cooldown press follows a recall a median 0.70
seconds later, and 84% within one second [[1]](#ref-1).

## Opening a pull

There is no fixed opener. The first press of a boss pull is Howling Blast in
17 of 56 pulls, Empower Rune Weapon in 15 and Obliterate in 8
[[1]](#ref-1). Trash pulls open with Empower Rune Weapon or Howling Blast.

1. **Howling Blast** at range to tag the pack, or **Empower Rune Weapon**
2. The Pillar press, if it is ready. See [The burst window](#the-burst-window)
3. Then the priority list

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In 24 of 56 boss pulls, Pillar of Frost was still on cooldown from the trash
> before the boss. The first Pillar on a boss arrived a median 6.4 seconds into
> the fight [[1]](#ref-1). A written opener that assumes cooldowns are ready
> fits fewer than 6 in 10 boss pulls in a real key.

## Four rules that surprise

A written priority list does not say these plainly. The logs support each one
strongly.

**Howling Blast is a Rime button only.** 98% of Howling Blasts are cast with
Rime up, with a range of 90% to 100% across the 16 logs [[1]](#ref-1). Without
Rime it costs a Rune for little damage.

**Obliterate goes first at 2 Killing Machine stacks.** Killing Streak makes
Obliterate and Frostscythe consume every stack at once and pay a bonus for each
[[10]](#ref-10). With 2 stacks up, the field presses Obliterate 79% of the time
[[1]](#ref-1). Overall, 93% of Obliterates and 93% of Frostscythes are cast
with Killing Machine up.

**Exterminate is not a button.** The Exterminate scythe appears as a cast in
the log, 3.2 times a minute. It fires a steady 0.52 seconds after the
Obliterate or Frostscythe before it, in 1,411 of 1,454 casts [[1]](#ref-1).
Reaper's Mark grants the buff that arms it, through Echoing Fury
[[8]](#ref-8).

**Empower Rune Weapon costs no global cooldown in practice.** The next global
cooldown press follows it a median 0.66 seconds later, and within 0.3 seconds
in 15% of cases [[1]](#ref-1). The spell page lists a 750-millisecond global
cooldown [[3]](#ref-3), and the logs do not show one. Press it between other
buttons, 4.3 times a minute on bosses.

## Between burst windows

The rotation slows when the burst ends. Rates below are casts per minute,
medians across the 16 logs, inside boss and trash pulls [[1]](#ref-1).

| | Breath up | Pillar only | Neither |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Global cooldown presses** | 55.8 | 48.7 | 37.2 |
| **Median gap between presses** | 1.03 s | 1.21 s | 1.24 s |
| Obliterate | 11.5 | 13.3 | 7.6 |
| Frostscythe | 13.0 | 10.0 | 5.8 |
| Howling Blast | 9.9 | 7.7 | 9.1 |
| Frost Strike | 6.5 | 6.3 | 7.9 |
| Glacial Advance | 8.4 | 4.3 | 6.2 |
| Empower Rune Weapon | 5.8 | 5.3 | 3.6 |

Outside both windows, 62% of combat time, Frost Strike and Howling Blast carry
more of the rotation, and Obliterate drops by a third. Rune spenders need
Killing Machine, and fewer procs arrive outside Pillar.

> [!NOTE]
> The gap between presses changes much less than the per-minute rate. The
> median gap outside the windows is 1.24 seconds, and 69% of gaps are 1.3
> seconds or less [[1]](#ref-1). The lower rate comes mostly from movement and
> short breaks between packs. Outside the burst the field still presses a
> button on nearly every global cooldown.

## Troubleshooting

Work through these when the rotation feels empty or the damage is low. The
first two are the common causes.

1. **Howling Blast without Rime.** Every one of these costs a Rune that an
   Obliterate needed. The field casts 98% of Howling Blasts with Rime
   [[1]](#ref-1).
2. **Empower Rune Weapon held.** The field presses it 4.3 times a minute on
   bosses, a median 13.3 seconds apart [[1]](#ref-1). The Killing Machine state
   at the press varies from log to log: 3% to 59% of presses come with no stack
   up. Treat it as a button to press whenever a charge is ready, not one to save.
3. **Pillar of Frost held for Breath.** Pillar goes out every 45 seconds, and
   only every second press includes Breath. See
   [The Pillar-only press](#the-pillar-only-press).
4. **Frostwyrm's Fury outside a Breath window.** The field casts it only with
   Breath. The recall comes about 3.5 seconds later, not at the end of the
   window.
5. **Frostscythe on two or three enemies.** The field casts it for 3% of Rune
   spenders at that size. See [When to swap](#when-to-swap).
6. **Interrupts missed.** Mind Freeze runs from 0.46 to 1.67 casts a minute, a
   3.6-times spread and 14 to 44 per key [[1]](#ref-1). No damage button in
   the sample has a spread close to that.

## Buttons not pressed

Several abilities that written guides mention have zero casts in all 16 logs.
Do not look for them on the bars.

- **Remorseless Winter** — Pillar of Frost casts it through Frozen Dominion,
  which every top player takes [[12]](#ref-12)[[30]](#ref-30)
- **Horn of Winter** — now a passive that returns 2 Runes [[14]](#ref-14)
- **Death and Decay** — zero casts
- **Chill Streak**, **Soul Reaper** and **Abomination Limb** — absent from the
  top-50 talent picks [[30]](#ref-30)
- **Exterminate** — fires automatically. See
  [Four rules that surprise](#four-rules-that-surprise)
- **Frostbane** and **Shattering Blade** — builds that need Razorice. No
  sampled player used Razorice

**Death Strike** is a self-heal, not a rotational cast. It appears in 12 of 16
logs, 82 casts in all [[1]](#ref-1).

## Hero talent

Play **Deathbringer**. 47 of 48 checked candidates at key level 16 to 18 cast
Reaper's Mark 26 to 35 times per key [[1]](#ref-1). The one exception cast it
twice, which suggests Rider of the Apocalypse or a mid-key swap.

Deathbringer adds one button, Reaper's Mark, on the same 45-second cooldown as
Pillar of Frost [[7]](#ref-7). Its other nodes are passive. The ones that
matter for the rotation:

- **Grim Reaper** — the mark's first hit grants Killing Machine [[8]](#ref-8)
- **Echoing Fury** — casting Reaper's Mark grants one Exterminate
  [[8]](#ref-8)
- **Exterminate** — the next Obliterates or Frostscythes cost 1 Rune and add a
  scythe strike. The first scythe always grants Killing Machine [[8]](#ref-8)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nothing in this file is tested for Rider of the Apocalypse. The sample holds
> no Rider log.

## Without the tier set

The patch 12.1 set bonuses touch Remorseless Winter only [[13]](#ref-13):

- **2-piece** — each Remorseless Winter hit grants Freezing Tempest, 1% attack
  speed and 2% Icy Death Torrent damage for 5 seconds, stacking
- **4-piece** — Remorseless Winter hits 25% more often, and Freezing Tempest
  lasts 5 seconds longer

Frozen Dominion makes Pillar of Frost cast Remorseless Winter
[[12]](#ref-12), so neither bonus adds or removes a press. A player without
the set uses the same lists, the same burst press and the same macros. Only the
damage is lower.

> [!CAUTION]
> Two community claims about the set are unverified. One player estimates the
> set at "around 25%" of Mythic+ damage, with no working shown
> [[32]](#ref-32). The same thread says a two-handed weapon is fine until the
> set arrives and worse with it [[32]](#ref-32). All 16 sampled players
> dual-wield and wear the set, so the logs cannot test a two-handed build.

## What to bind

Two presses carry the burst: the Pillar press and the Breath press. The table
measures how often each cast lands within 200 milliseconds of Pillar of Frost,
close enough to share one keypress [[1]](#ref-1).

| Ability | Within 0.2 s of Pillar | Global cooldown | Same press? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raise Dead | 84% | — | **Yes** |
| Voracious Heart of Ula'tek | 70% | — | **Yes**, Breath press only |
| Breath of Sindragosa | 68% | Off [[4]](#ref-4) | **Yes**, Breath press only |
| Reaper's Mark | 58% | 1.5 s [[7]](#ref-7) | **Yes** |
| Potion of Recklessness | 37% | — | Optional |
| Empower Rune Weapon | 28% | No, in practice | No — own key |

Pillar of Frost costs no global cooldown [[2]](#ref-2), and neither does
Breath [[4]](#ref-4). Reaper's Mark is the only cast in the press that does,
so the press fits in one macro.

> [!NOTE]
> The Raise Dead global cooldown was not checked on its spell page. The logs
> show it inside the same 200 milliseconds as Pillar and Reaper's Mark in most
> windows, which is not possible if it cost a full global cooldown.

## Macros

These macros come from the logged timings, not from a guide. Each states the
observation that supports it. Paste each block exactly as shown.

### Pillar press

Press this every 45 seconds when Breath of Sindragosa is not ready.

```
#showtooltip Pillar of Frost
/cast Pillar of Frost
/cast Reaper's Mark
```

> [!NOTE]
> **Why:** Reaper's Mark lands within 0.5 seconds of Pillar in 375 of 497
> windows, and at a median offset of 0.0 seconds [[1]](#ref-1). Icy Veins gives
> the same advice: "macro it with your Pillar of Frost every time"
> [[19]](#ref-19).

### Breath press

Press this every 90 seconds, when Pillar and Breath are both ready. Press
Empower Rune Weapon just before it.

```
#showtooltip Breath of Sindragosa
/cast Pillar of Frost
/cast Breath of Sindragosa
/cast Raise Dead
/use 13
/use 14
/cast Reaper's Mark
```

`13` is the top trinket slot and `14` the bottom one. Delete the line for a
passive trinket. Keep Reaper's Mark on the last line, because it is the only
line that costs a global cooldown.

> [!WARNING]
> Breath of Sindragosa costs 60 Runic Power [[4]](#ref-4). If the macro fires
> with less, Breath fails silently and the rest of the press still goes out.
> Empower Rune Weapon gives 40 Runic Power [[3]](#ref-3), which is why the
> field presses it 0.1 seconds before this macro in 98% of Breath windows
> [[1]](#ref-1).

For a planned pull, add the potion. The field uses 63 of 68 potions inside a
Breath window [[1]](#ref-1), so keep it on a second copy of this macro rather
than on every Breath press.

```
#showtooltip Breath of Sindragosa
/cast Pillar of Frost
/cast Breath of Sindragosa
/cast Raise Dead
/use Potion of Recklessness
/use 13
/cast Reaper's Mark
```

### Interrupt on mouseover

Interrupting is the widest skill gap in the sample, so this macro is the one
most likely to improve a key.

```
#showtooltip Mind Freeze
/cast [@mouseover,harm,nodead][] Mind Freeze
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why:** all 16 logs cast Mind Freeze, but the rate runs from 0.46 to 1.67
> casts a minute, and 14 to 44 per key [[1]](#ref-1). A mouseover cast removes
> the target swap.

### Utility on mouseover

These cast on the unit under the cursor, and fall back to the current target.

```
#showtooltip Death Grip
/cast [@mouseover,harm,nodead][] Death Grip
```

```
#showtooltip Asphyxiate
/cast [@mouseover,harm,nodead][] Asphyxiate
```

```
#showtooltip Raise Ally
/cast [@mouseover,help,dead][] Raise Ally
```

### What not to macro

Three abilities look like they belong in the burst macro. The timings say
they do not.

- **Empower Rune Weapon** — only 28% land within 0.2 seconds of Pillar, and it
  is pressed 4.3 times a minute. It needs its own key.
- **Frostwyrm's Fury** — a median +2.0 seconds after Pillar, and it costs a
  global cooldown [[5]](#ref-5). In the Breath macro it would compete with
  Reaper's Mark, and one of the two would be dropped with no error.
- **The recall** — a median 3.5 seconds after Frostwyrm's Fury. It is the same
  button as Frostwyrm's Fury, so the same key does both.

> [!CAUTION]
> A macro cannot fire two abilities that both cost a global cooldown. The game
> drops one of them silently. Reaper's Mark and Frostwyrm's Fury are the two
> global cooldown casts near the burst, so keep them on separate keys.

## Where logs beat guides

On these points the logs contradict a current written guide. In each case this
file follows the logs.

**Frostscythe does not start at two targets.** Maxroll and the Wowhead talent
page say to swap to Frostscythe at 2 targets [[29]](#ref-29)[[24]](#ref-24).
Icy Veins, Method and the Wowhead rotation page say 3 [[19]](#ref-19)[[26]](#ref-26)[[23]](#ref-23).
On pulls with two or three enemies the field casts Frostscythe for 3% of Rune
spenders, and only 25% at four to six [[1]](#ref-1).

**Recall Frostwyrm's Fury at once.** Icy Veins recalls it about 12 seconds
later in the same Pillar [[19]](#ref-19). Method's rotation page recalls it
near the end of Pillar, and its talent page holds it for the next Pillar
[[26]](#ref-26)[[27]](#ref-27). Maxroll waits for the Haste buff to expire
[[29]](#ref-29). The logs show a median 3.5 seconds after the first cast, and
95% inside the same Pillar [[1]](#ref-1).

**Reaper's Mark grants Exterminate, not Frostwyrm's Fury.** Maxroll and Method
say Frostwyrm's Fury grants the Echoing Fury stack [[29]](#ref-29)[[27]](#ref-27).
The tooltip says Reaper's Mark [[8]](#ref-8). The logs show 357 Exterminate
buffs applied by a Reaper's Mark cast and 33 by Frostwyrm's Fury
[[1]](#ref-1). This file follows the tooltip and the logs.

**Empower Rune Weapon is off the global cooldown.** The spell page lists 750
milliseconds [[3]](#ref-3). Icy Veins and Method say it is off the global
cooldown [[19]](#ref-19)[[26]](#ref-26). The logs agree with the guides. See
[Four rules that surprise](#four-rules-that-surprise).

**The Mythic+ build is Breath, not Frostbane.** Method labels its Mythic+ build
Frostbane and describes Razorice on the main hand. Its import string is the one
Icy Veins and Wowhead call the Breath build [[27]](#ref-27)[[21]](#ref-21)[[24]](#ref-24).
No sampled player used Razorice, and all 16 cast Breath of Sindragosa
[[1]](#ref-1).

> [!CAUTION]
> Icy Veins and Method share one author, so they are not independent sources.
> Maxroll's page was last updated on 17 August 2026, and it carries old values
> for several Deathbringer talents and for the tier set [[29]](#ref-29).

## The sample

Read the limits before relying on any number. This is what the numbers rest
on [[1]](#ref-1).

- **16 Mythic+ keys**, 2 from each of the 8 Season 2 dungeons
- **Key levels 16 to 18** — 7 at level 18, 7 at 17 and 2 at 16. Drawn from
  ranking pages 6 and 14, not page 1. The DPS band is 201k to 337k
- **Four regions** — US 5, EU 5, CN 4, TW 2
- **16 of 16 Deathbringer**, dual-wield, Breath of Sindragosa, 4-piece tier
  set
- 56 boss pulls (182 minutes), 107 trash pulls of 4 or more enemies (229
  minutes), and 10 single-enemy boss pulls (32 minutes)
- 498 Pillar of Frost windows, 222 of them with Breath, and 26,099 cast events

What the sample does **not** cover:

- **Rider of the Apocalypse** — zero logs
- **A two-handed weapon** or a **Frostbane** build — zero logs
- **No tier set** — zero logs. The buttons do not change, but the rates may
- **Rune and Runic Power counts** at each press — not in the cast data, so the
  1-stack Killing Machine rule stays unconfirmed
- **An exact target-count threshold** — enemy counts are cumulative per pull
- **Key level 19 and above**, and raid
