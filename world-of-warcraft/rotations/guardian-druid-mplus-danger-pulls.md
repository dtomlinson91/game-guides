# Guardian Druid — Mythic+ danger pulls

A pull-by-pull defensive plan for three Mythic+ pulls that kill Guardian
Druids, for Midnight, patch 12.1, Season 2. It shows what strong tanks press
in the first seconds of each pull and how they cover it to the end. Every
time, share and damage value comes from 24 timed key level 21 runs on
Warcraft Logs, 8 per dungeon [[1]](#ref-1).

- [Murder Row](#murder-row-the-punisher-pack) — the pack with a Shivan
  Punisher straight after the second boss
- [Voidscar Arena](#voidscar-arena-the-brutalizer-pulls) — the three pulls
  with a Devouring Brutalizer
- [Kings' Rest](#kings-rest-the-opening-guardians) — the two Animated
  Guardians at the start, the next pack, and Bloodlust

> [!NOTE]
> This guide builds on the
> [Guardian defensives guide](guardian-druid-mplus-defensives.md), which
> explains each defensive, its real cooldown and the general big-pull cycle
> [[2]](#ref-2). All 24 tanks played Elune's Chosen with the 4-piece set.
> Barkskin lasts 14 seconds on a 34-second cooldown, Incarnation 35 seconds,
> Lunar Beam 11.5 seconds on a 40-second cooldown, and Survival Instincts 6
> seconds on 2 charges.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Time 0 is the arrival.** Warcraft Logs merges chained packs into one
> pull, so this guide splits them by when each enemy first damages a player.
> A gap of more than 5 seconds between arrivals starts a new wave. A melee
> enemy that runs in can take a second or two to land its first hit.

## One rule for all three

Every one of these pulls is most dangerous in its first 10 seconds. The
tanks arrive with almost no Ironfur, at a median of 0.7 stacks in the first 5
seconds on Murder Row and 0.8 on Kings' Rest. Melee hits land
hardest exactly then.

| Ironfur stacks at the hit | Shivan Punisher | Wrathguard Flayer | Animated Guardian | Devouring Brutalizer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 26% | 27% | 33% | 16% |
| 1 | 14% | 11% | 21% | 7% |
| 3 | 8% | 7% | 10% | 5% |
| 5 | 6% | 5% | 8% | 4% |
| 7 | 4% | 4% | 7% | 4% |

Values are the median melee hit after mitigation, as a share of maximum
health. On Murder Row, stacks reach about 2.5 by 5 to 10 seconds and 5 by 15
seconds.

That is why the strong tanks press **Barkskin at contact**. On these pulls
it was the first major in most logs, pressed between 2.3 seconds before and
0.5 seconds after the arrival. It covers the 14 seconds while Ironfur
builds.

> [!TIP]
> Spend the first Rage of a dangerous pull on Ironfur. Each of the first
> three stacks cuts melee damage more than any later stack does.

## Murder Row: the Punisher pack

The first pack after Zaen Bladesorrow, the second boss. No tank died on it
in the sample, but every tank dropped to between 10% and 49% health. Only 2
of the 8 groups used Bloodlust on it, at 7 and 19 seconds.

### What arrives

The first wave arrives together, within about 5 to 9 seconds:

- 1 Shivan Punisher
- 2 to 3 Wrathguard Flayers
- 2 to 3 Fel Invokers
- 2 to 4 Trained Felhunters
- 2 to 3 Unleashed Imps

What joins later depends on the route:

- **Demon Flies** in groups of 4 to 8, at 28 to 129 seconds.
- **A second group** of 1 Fel Invoker, 2 Felhunters, 1 Flayer and 1 Imp, at
  7 to 64 seconds.
- **A Corrupted Warlock with 2 Flayers**, at 48 to 120 seconds.

The pack lasted a median of 80 seconds, with a range of 51 to 130.

### The first 20 seconds

| Press | Tanks | When, median | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barkskin | 8 of 8 | 0.3 s **before** arrival | 2.3 s before to 5.7 s after |
| Lunar Beam | 8 of 8 | 4.6 s | 2.0 to 20.8 s |
| Incarnation | 8 of 8 | 5.7 s | 0.6 s before to 24.6 s after |
| Survival Instincts | 4 of 8 | 26.4 s | 7.7 to 60.7 s |

- **Barkskin was already up at the arrival on 5 of 8 pulls.** The tank pressed
  it while walking in.
- **Five tanks pressed Incarnation within 6.6 seconds,** and all five had
  Lunar Beam out by 10.8 seconds. The other three delayed Incarnation to 14.5
  to 24.6 seconds, into Barkskin's second half.
- **The opener stacks here, unlike a normal pull.** From 5 to 15 seconds,
  Barkskin was up on 7 to 8 tanks, and Incarnation and Lunar Beam on 5 to 7.

at 23% to 95% health. Two tanks used a potion and one a Healthstone. Five of
8 cast Soothe during the pack, first at 16 to 67 seconds.

### What hurts

| Source | Share of damage to the tank |
| --- | --- |
| Trained Felhunter, Shadow Bite | 22% |
| Wrathguard Flayer, melee | 20% |
| Shivan Punisher, melee | 19% |
| Trained Felhunter, melee | 15% |
| Shivan Punisher, Demonic Frenzy | 7% |
| Fel Invoker, melee | 4% |

- **The Punisher's opening melee is the killer.** Its hits in the first 10
  seconds had a median of 15% of max health and a maximum of 44%. After 10
  seconds the median fell to 5% to 9%. Of the 8 tanks' 5 largest hits each,
  29 of 40 were Punisher melee. The largest melee hits, 43% and 44%, landed
  in the first second.
- **Barkskin halves the Punisher's melee.** The median hit was 5.6% under
  Barkskin, 8.6% under Incarnation alone, 10.0% with nothing up, and 3.5%
  under Survival Instincts.
- **Shadow Bite is the steady damage.** The 2 to 4 Felhunters start it 4.6 to
  8.2 seconds in, at about 6% per hit. Armor reduces it only in part.
  Barkskin brings it to 5% and Survival Instincts to 3%, against 9% with
  nothing up.
- **Demonic Frenzy** pulses about once a second for about 20 seconds,
  starting 24 to 46 seconds in, at 4% to 6% per tick.
- **Whirlwind** from the Punisher hit 2 tanks once each, at 9.6 to 9.9
  seconds, for 18% and 46%.

## Voidscar Arena: the Brutalizer pulls

Every sampled route had exactly three pulls with a Devouring Brutalizer, all
between Atroxus and Charonus. No tank died in any of them, and no group used
Bloodlust on them. The lowest health per tank ranged from 8% to 70%.

### Brutalize

Brutalize decides these pulls. It is a channel of **5 hits, 1.5 seconds
apart, about 6 seconds in all**.

- **The first channel lands 12.6 to 21.0 seconds after the arrival** in 22
  of 24 pulls, at a median of 16.6 seconds.
- **It repeats about every 28 to 31 seconds:** at a median of 45.5 seconds,
  then about 74 and 101 seconds if the Brutalizer lives that long.
- **Each hit is physical and huge.** Before armor it is about 190% of max
  health. After armor it is 24% with nothing up.
- **Survival Instincts lasts 6 seconds, exactly one channel.**

What each major did to a Brutalize hit, across 248 hits:

| Strongest major up | Hits | Median per hit | Largest hit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Survival Instincts | 38 | 9.5% | 20% |
| Barkskin | 91 | 17% | 36% |
| Lunar Beam only | 30 | 22.5% | 47% |
| Nothing | 29 | 24% | 48% |
| Incarnation only | 60 | 24.5% | 54% |

Per channel, the total of 5 hits was a median of **51%** of max health
under Survival Instincts, against 83% under Incarnation and 95% under
Barkskin on the first channel. Incarnation raises maximum health but does
not reduce damage, so it lets the tank survive a channel without making it
smaller.

**Dreadbellow** is the other Brutalizer damage. It is a pulse about once a
second for 6% of max health per tick, and armor barely reduces it. It made up
14% to 17% of the damage to the tank in each pull.

### The plan

The strong tanks covered every first channel with a major. Not one first
channel in 22 landed with nothing up.

| Brutalize channel | Starts, median | Covered by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | 16.6 s | Incarnation 8, Survival Instincts 7, Barkskin 4, Lunar Beam 3 |
| Second | 45.5 s | Barkskin 13, Survival Instincts 3, Lunar Beam 2, Incarnation 2 |
| Third | 73.8 s | Barkskin 2, Lunar Beam 1, nothing 1 |

1. **0 seconds.** Barkskin at contact. It covers the low-Ironfur window and
   runs out at about 14 seconds, just before the first Brutalize.
2. **5 to 15 seconds.** Lunar Beam, then Incarnation.
3. **About 16 seconds.** Survival Instincts on the first Brutalize, if
   Incarnation is not enough. In pull 1, 4 of the 7 Survival Instincts
   presses came 14 to 21 seconds in. In pull 3, 3 of 4 came at 16.3 to 17.5
   seconds.
4. **About 45 seconds.** The second Barkskin on the second Brutalize. Its
   34-second cooldown brings it back in time.

> [!TIP]
> Barkskin at contact and Survival Instincts at the first Brutalize do not
> overlap. Barkskin ends at about 14 seconds, and the first channel starts at
> about 16.

## Kings' Rest: the opening Guardians

The first pull of the key. One other player died in it in 1 of the 8 logs,
and no tank died. The lowest health per tank ranged from 9% to 35%, which makes it
the most dangerous of the three pulls for the tank.

### The sequence

All 8 logs follow the same order.

| Seconds from arrival | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | The 2 Animated Guardians arrive, 19 to 26 seconds after the key starts. Their opening melee lands at once |
| 0–5 | The tank takes a median of 88% of max health in damage, and the group moves back |
| 5–15 | No damage taken on most logs. This is the short break |
| 15–20 | The Guardians re-engage |
| 18–33 | The next pack arrives: 2 to 6 Umbral Warriors, 1 to 2 Shadow-Borne Champions and usually a Risen Hexer |
| 20–32 | Bloodlust, in all 8 logs |
| 31–44 | The first Shadow Whirlwind from a Champion |
| 40–55 | Peak damage, at a median of 116% to 140% of max health per 5 seconds |
| 80–126 | The Guardians die |

### Incarnation before the key

Incarnation was **already up when the key started in all 8 logs**. It ended
27 to 28 seconds into the key, which is 2 to 9 seconds after the Guardians
arrived. All 8 tanks then pressed it again 42 to 52 seconds into the key.

That second press came well under Incarnation's 78.5-second cooldown floor
[[2]](#ref-2). That fits a cooldown reset at key start, so a pre-key
Incarnation costs nothing. The logs cannot show the reset itself, only the
gap.

> [!TIP]
> Press Incarnation a few seconds before the key starts. It covers the
> Guardians' opening hits, and the full Incarnation is back for the second
> engagement and Bloodlust.

### The opener

| Press | Tanks | When, median | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Incarnation (pre-key) | 8 of 8 | Up at arrival | Ends 2 to 9 s after arrival |
| Barkskin | 8 of 8 | 0.1 s before | 1.5 s before to 26.0 s after |
| Lunar Beam | 8 of 8 | 19.6 s | 9.3 to 37.1 s |
| Incarnation (second) | 8 of 8 | 21.7 s | 16.8 to 33.0 s |
| Survival Instincts | 8 of 8 | 45.3 s | 18.8 to 84.4 s |

- **Barkskin at contact on 6 of 8.** The other two held it to 16 and 26
  seconds, for the re-engage.
- **Lunar Beam and Incarnation go out around Bloodlust.** All 8 tanks pressed
  both between 9 and 37 seconds. On 4 of 8 they were about 2 seconds apart
  or less.
- **Half the tanks spent a Survival Instincts charge at 18.8 to 25.1
  seconds,** as the Guardians and the second pack hit together. They pressed
  it at 43% to 100% health.

> [!WARNING]
> **15 to 20 seconds is the weak point.** Only 38% of tanks had a major up
> in that slice. Barkskin from the opener has ended, the pre-key Incarnation
> has ended, and the second Incarnation is not yet out. Lowest health in that
> slice reached 21%. Press Lunar Beam or Incarnation as the Guardians
> re-engage, not after.

### Soothe the Guardians

**All 8 tanks cast Soothe during this pull,** 1 to 3 times each. Soothe is
the Druid's Enrage removal.

- **First Soothe:** 40.5 to 46.3 seconds on 6 of 8 tanks. The other two
  first cast it at 73.6 and 107.2 seconds.
- **Second Soothe:** about 25 seconds later, at 65 to 74 seconds, on 6 of 8.
- **Third Soothe:** at about 91 seconds, on the tanks still fighting.

The logs do not carry enemy buffs, so they cannot show the Enrage itself.
The regular 25-second spacing suggests it returns on that interval. Guardian
melee was largest in the first 15 seconds, at a median of 117% to 135% of
max health per hit before mitigation on 5 of 8 logs, and about 90% to 110%
after that.

### Shadow Whirlwind

The Shadow-Borne Champion's Shadow Whirlwind is the largest single hit after
the opening Guardian melee.

- **First cast at 31 to 44 seconds,** about 10 seconds after the Champion
  arrives, then about every 24 seconds.
- **A median of 33.5% of max health per hit,** with a maximum of 42%.
- **Armor reduces it only a little,** and Incarnation does nothing to it:
  the median was 37% under Incarnation. Barkskin brought it to 21.5% and
  Survival Instincts to 14%.

## How to read a timeline

Each row lists one tank's presses in order, in seconds from the arrival. A
negative number is a press before the pack arrived.

| Code | Button |
| --- | --- |
| B | Barkskin |
| I | Incarnation: Guardian of Ursoc |
| LB | Lunar Beam |
| SI | Survival Instincts, with the tank's health at the press |
| Soo | Soothe |
| Pot | Health potion |
| HS | Healthstone |
| FR | Frenzied Regeneration casts in the pull, counted separately |

Health at a press comes from a snapshot on the cast event and can lag a hit
by a fraction of a second.

## The sample

- **24 timed keys at level 21,** 8 per dungeon. Two per dungeon come from
  the middle of the key 21 ranking, as in the defensives guide. The other
  six per dungeon are the top six key 21 runs by tanks not already sampled.
  They were played between 18 September and 1 October 2026.
- **Tanks.** 16 distinct tanks. EU 19 logs, CN 3, US 2. Several strong tanks
  appear in more than one dungeon.
- **Per log:** the tank's casts and buffs, all damage taken with health and
  active auras on each hit, deaths, and every Bloodlust-type cast in the
  group.
- **Pull windows.** Each window starts at the first arrival of the wave that
  holds the named enemy. It ends at the named enemy's last damage, or the
  first wave's last damage on Murder Row.
- **Limits.** The sample holds strong tanks only, and every one survived
  these pulls. It shows a plan that works, not the mistake behind a death.
  Enemy buffs, such as the Guardian Enrage, are not in the data.
